Academic Projects

My studies involve geographic data processing, spatial analysis and interactive mapping.

EV Charging and Micromobility Site Selection

Our graduation project explored suitable locations for electric vehicle charging stations and micromobility parking areas in Çankaya, Ankara. We used GIS, the Analytic Hierarchy Process (AHP) and weighted overlay to evaluate location suitability.

LiDAR Surface Modelling

Processing LiDAR point clouds to generate a Digital Terrain Model (DTM), Digital Surface Model (DSM) and Canopy Height Model (CHM).

Personal Web GIS Portfolio

Building a multi-page website with HTML and CSS, with interactive OpenLayers and Leaflet maps.
